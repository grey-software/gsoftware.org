Grey Software funds people doing quiet, useful work that large institutions pass over. Every amount here is in US dollars.

2020 to now

Open source maintainers

Open source infrastructure is often kept up by individuals doing quiet, essential work. Since 2020 we have sent recurring support through GitHub Sponsors to maintainers who keep useful software free for everyone.

$3,128 to date, with eight sponsorships active at about $139 a month.

2026, over four months

Pashto language models

We awarded a research grant over four months to Junaid Ahmad, who builds language models and speech recognition for Pashto and other underserved languages in Pakistan. It builds on earlier support: two months of Colab Pro and a month of Hugging Face Pro.

$600 over four months.

Work this specific and this local rarely gets funded by large institutions. That is why it belongs here.

June 2026

Code for Pakistan hackathon

We partnered with Code for Pakistan for their civic tech hackathon, with a direct donation and Scrimba subscriptions we arranged as prizes for the winning teams. Grey Software was named as a sponsor to participants throughout.

$300, and $1,000 in Scrimba subscriptions.
