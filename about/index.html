Open software. Open education.

Grey Software is a registered Canadian nonprofit that makes open software and learning tools.

  • Our grey matter is the work.

    Checking an answer before you trust it. Understanding a thing well enough to change it. Tools can draft, and the judging stays ours.

  • A nonprofit, not a charity.

    Grey Software does not exist to turn a profit. We are not against large companies or AI. We use both, and push back on the shortcut that skips the understanding.

  • Fewer things, finished.

    Each tool does one job for the mind and does it carefully. Four are live as alphas, and they get better in public.

  • Built for the edges.

    Distance should not decide who gets good tools. We build for people far from where the decisions are made, learning mostly on their own.

  • Good work by others gets a shelf of its own.

    We point to what others build that we believe in, and we never slip our own apps in among it.
